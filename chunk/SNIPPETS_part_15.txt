## 8. Known assumptions & risks (flagged rather than hidden)

1. **`Arm.y`** — `INPUT_Y_OFFSETS = [1, 0]` in `factorySim.ts` is a parity guess against
   `drops.takeAt(ix+0.5-dir[0], arm.y, iz+0.5-dir[1], 0.52)`. Confirm the arm's pivot height and
   drop the fallback; a wrong guess makes off-planet inserters *slightly more* capable than live
   ones (they'd also grab from the belt level), which shows up as a drift in `parity().inserted`.
2. **`B.PLANK` vs `B.PLANKS`** — the `FUELS` table in §4.10 keys off a `B.PLANK` alias defined in
   `fps/World.ts`. If the alias is ever dropped, furnace fuel silently stops working *both*
   live and headless; unify on `B.PLANKS`.
3. **Belt lanes are rebuilt whole** on any conveyor change (`beltsDirty`). At 32×32 chunks that's
   microseconds; if belt counts ever reach 1e5, switch to incremental lane splicing.
4. **`prepareAllData` keeps all 1024 chunk `Uint8Array`s resident (20 MB).** The sim's headless
   view therefore only matters while the planet is unloaded; its LRU is capped at 24 chunks
   (~0.5 MB per inactive planet). Ten remembered planets ≈ 5 MB — deliberate.
5. **Offline catch-up is capped at 8 h** (`MAX_OFFLINE_SEC`). A week away will not mint a week of
   ore. Raise it only after measuring `advance()` cost with real machine counts.
6. **Fluids and enemies remain live-only** — stated in code comments in `factorySim.ts` and
   `universeSim.ts` so the scope decision is discoverable, not folklore.
