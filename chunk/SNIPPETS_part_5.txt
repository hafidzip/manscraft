## 0. The five decisions that make this work

| # | Decision | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Machine *placements* need no separate save.** Every machine block arrives through `world.setBlock` → `ChangeBus.onBlock` → the delta store. Restoring the delta restores the machines; only *runtime* state (arm phase, held item, laser charge) is snapshotted by the sim. | Kills a whole class of two‑source‑of‑truth bugs. |
| 2 | **`ensureData` must pass `fromGenerator = (appliedEdits === 0)`.** `MachineRegistry` skips its rescan when `fromGenerator===true` (§4.6 trust‑generator fast path). If we replay a delta containing conveyors and still claim "from generator", **those machines are never indexed**. | Without this, Feature A silently restores machine *blocks* with no machine *behaviour*. |
| 3 | **Deltas are applied *before* `buildColumnCache`**, not just before `onChunkData`. | `colH/colC/colW` are derived caches; feeding them pre‑edit voxels produces wrong lighting/water columns for restored chunks. |
| 4 | **Ledger invariant: one unit is EITHER a ledger count OR a physical `DroppedItem`, never both.** `spawn` checks out; evict/sleep‑exit checks back in; nothing is ever destroyed. | Makes conservation (`in − out === ledger + physical`) a testable console assertion. |
| 5 | **Off‑planet simulation carries fractional remainders** (`charge -= 1`, `t -= CYCLE`) instead of resetting. | 10 minutes away ⇒ exactly 10 minutes of production, even when catch‑up runs in 1‑second quanta. |

Tick order inside one sim step is fixed and load‑bearing: **miners → belts → inserters → furnaces**.
(Mine first so fresh drops ride the belt in the same step; inserters after belts so an item that just
arrived at a lane terminus is grabbable this step, matching the live frame order
`itemDrops.update → machines.update → updateFurnaces`.)
